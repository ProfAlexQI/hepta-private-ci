This paper establishes theoretical foundations for the Neural Differential Utility (NDU) framework where information is driven by general multi-dimensional squareintegrable martingales. We define the core NDU system as a forward-backward stochastic differential equation (FBSDE) where the forward component, representing the agent's preference state, and the backward component, representing the utility aggregator, are driven by distinct components of the general martingale. We provide rigorous conditions for the existence and uniqueness of solutions to this FBSDE system. Furthermore, we address the problem of optimal control of the preference parameters. Under specific regularity, growth, concavity, and a crucial weak-to-strong continuity assumption for the FBSDE solution map, we prove the existence of an optimal control.
