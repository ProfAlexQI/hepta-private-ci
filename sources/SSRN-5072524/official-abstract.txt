This paper introduces Neural Differential Utility (NDU), a novel framework integrating deep neural networks within a recursive utility paradigm. NDU offers a tractable yet more granular approach for analyzing the evolution of preferences within a resource-constrained agent. We establish conditions for NDU to satisfy the Universal Approximation Theorem, demonstrating its capacity to endogenize various preferences within L p spaces. In a representative agent asset market setting, NDU generates nonlinear and time-varying SDF, shining light the theoretical foundations for the growing applications of deep learning in empirical asset pricing.
