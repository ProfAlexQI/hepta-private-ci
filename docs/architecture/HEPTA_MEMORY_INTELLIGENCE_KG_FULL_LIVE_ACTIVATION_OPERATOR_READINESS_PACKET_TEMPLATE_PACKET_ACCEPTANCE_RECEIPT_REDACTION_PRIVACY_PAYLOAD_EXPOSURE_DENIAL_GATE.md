# Hepta Memory/Intelligence/KG Full Live Activation Operator Readiness Packet Template Packet Acceptance Receipt Redaction Privacy Payload Exposure Denial Gate

This gate prevents non-persistent packet acceptance receipts from being turned
into payload exposure, privacy review, redaction bypass, secret scan, plaintext
materialization, or authority-bearing privacy evidence.

It consumes the packet acceptance receipt export/query/observability denial
report and models sixteen redaction/privacy/payload-exposure surfaces:

- redacted payload preview claim
- payload hash preview claim
- payload diff claim
- readback text claim
- operator summary text claim
- privacy review claim
- secret scan claim
- PII scan claim
- raw payload inspection claim
- plaintext materialization claim
- redaction bypass claim
- hash-to-payload link claim
- external redaction review claim
- privacy acceptance claim
- authority from redaction claim
- live from privacy claim

Every surface is denied. No redacted payload preview, payload hash preview,
payload diff, readback text, operator summary text, privacy review, secret scan,
PII scan, raw payload inspection, plaintext materialization, redaction bypass,
hash-to-payload link, external redaction review, privacy acceptance, approval,
authority, command, or live execution is recorded, persisted, performed,
materialized, delivered, or accepted.

The gate does not mutate Memory/KG, attach intelligence context, invoke
providers/models, read credentials, install or restart services, mutate active
binaries, publish artifacts, make public claims, or send externally.
