# Hepta Memory/Intelligence/KG Full Live Activation Operator Readiness Packet Template Packet Acceptance Receipt Release Publication Result Receipt Final Operator Acknowledgement Non Acceptance Denial Gate

This gate prevents a denied release/publication result receipt from becoming a
final operator acknowledgement. It closes the path where operator-facing text
could be followed by "received", "confirmed", "read", "seen", final response,
or acknowledgement wording and then be treated as durable acceptance,
publication authority, activation authority, or live execution approval.

It consumes the release/publication result receipt operator-facing
summary/briefing non-persistence denial report and models eighteen final
operator acknowledgement surfaces:

- final operator acknowledgement claim
- operator received claim
- operator confirmed claim
- operator read claim
- operator seen claim
- final response claim
- completion acknowledgement claim
- status acknowledgement claim
- summary acknowledgement claim
- briefing acknowledgement claim
- readback digest acknowledgement claim
- dashboard/notification acknowledgement claim
- channel acknowledgement claim
- external acknowledgement claim
- Telegram acknowledgement claim
- release/publication authority acknowledgement claim
- activation/live acknowledgement claim
- install/restart/active-binary acknowledgement claim

Every surface is denied. No final acknowledgement, received/confirmed/read/seen
state, final response, completion/status/summary/briefing/readback/dashboard
acknowledgement, channel delivery, Telegram send, external send, operator
acceptance, operator approval, release/publication authority, activation
authority, activation command, live execution, install, restart, launchd
mutation, or active-binary mutation is accepted, recorded, persisted,
materialized, delivered, performed, or derived.

The gate preserves the previous operator-facing summary/briefing and result
receipt boundaries: no summary, briefing, readback digest, final note,
notification, timeline entry, audit/privacy narrative, query/export/
observability exposure, result receipt persistence, release/public artifact,
public release claim, GA claim, Memory/KG write, provider/model invocation,
credential read, or secret read occurs.
