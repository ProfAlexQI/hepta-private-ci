# Hepta Memory/Intelligence/KG Full Live Activation Operator Readiness Packet Template Packet Acceptance Receipt Audit Trail Immutable Evidence Denial Gate

This gate prevents non-persistent packet acceptance receipts from being
wrapped as audit trails, immutable evidence, proof chains, attestations, or
ledger-backed authority.

It consumes the packet acceptance receipt cancellation/supersession denial
report and models sixteen audit/evidence surfaces:

- audit trail append claim
- immutable evidence claim
- hash chain claim
- Merkle root claim
- attestation claim
- witness claim
- notary claim
- ledger evidence claim
- index evidence claim
- delivery evidence claim
- export evidence claim
- query evidence claim
- observability evidence claim
- readback evidence claim
- authority evidence claim
- live evidence claim

Every surface is denied. No audit trail, immutable evidence, hash chain,
Merkle root, attestation, witness, notary, ledger/index/delivery/export/query/
observability evidence, readback evidence, acceptance, approval, authority,
command, or live execution is recorded, persisted, materialized, or accepted.

The gate does not mutate Memory/KG, attach intelligence context, invoke
providers/models, read credentials, install or restart services, mutate active
binaries, publish artifacts, make public claims, or send externally.
