# Hepta Intelligence session bootstrap

For every audit, design, implementation, review, qualification, or research session in this subtree:

1. Read `HEPTA_INTELLIGENCE_CURRENT_PLAN.json`.
2. Read `HEPTA_INTELLIGENCE_DOCUMENT_AUTHORITY_REGISTRY_V1.json`.
3. Read `HEPTA_INTELLIGENCE_EVIDENCE_INDEX_V1.json`; treat its entries as time-bounded evidence, not plan authority.
4. Read `HEPTA_INTELLIGENCE_MASTER_PLAN.md`.
5. Treat the master plan as the only current human-readable development plan and `CURRENT_PLAN.json` as the only current machine authority.
6. Treat versioned plans, tranche documents, PR bodies, receipts, snapshots, and chat summaries as historical evidence only.
7. Preserve every registered legacy machine compatibility contract until all consumers migrate through a paired, receipted adapter. Evidence-only does not mean safe to replace or truncate.
8. Fail closed when branch/head, digest, stage, claim, authority, document pointers, compatibility schemas, or evidence identity disagree.
9. Execute only the first unblocked action in the canonical plan.
10. While Q0 is active, do not add new runtime source, migrations, H5/H6/H7 runtime tranches, production callers, or CALLERS changes.
11. Distinguish PR-head evidence from merge-candidate evidence; a checkout step name alone is not proof of exact-head identity.
12. Do not claim self-evolution, lifelong learning efficacy, experience-shaped intuition, biomimetic neurons, structural plasticity, or neuromorphic behavior without the exact claim-level evidence defined by the master plan.
13. Update the stable master plan instead of creating another dated or versioned master roadmap.
